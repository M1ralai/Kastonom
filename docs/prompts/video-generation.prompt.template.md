## Scene Direction

Create a short vertical ecommerce ad that keeps the product clearly visible. Show the product in practical, attractive use. Use clean pacing, simple transitions, and a direct product-focused composition suitable for TikTok/Reels.

Placeholder scene beats:

1. Open with the product visible in the first second.
2. Show the product context or use case.
3. Reinforce the main benefit from the approved claim.
4. End with CTA and product visibility.

## Rules

- Do not invent product claims.
- Do not show unavailable features.
- Keep suitable for ecommerce advertising.
- Use short Turkish text overlays only if needed.
- Prioritize product visibility.
- Output vertical video suitable for TikTok/Reels unless changed by contract.
- Use only facts present in the creative brief.
- Respect forbidden claims and negative instructions.

## Negative Instructions

Do not include:

- Unsupported discounts
- Unsupported guarantees
- Fake scarcity
- Medical, legal, financial, or safety claims not present in source data
- Unavailable product variants
- Provider watermarks unless unavoidable
- Long text overlays
