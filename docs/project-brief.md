# Kastonom Project Brief

## Project Name

Kastonom

## One-Line Summary

Kastonom helps ecommerce SMEs generate AI ad videos without prompt engineering by converting ERP, product, customer, sales, stock, and trend data into creative decisions.

## Problem

Small and medium ecommerce teams often need video ads but lack the time, budget, or prompt-engineering skill to brief AI video tools effectively. Product, stock, and sales signals already exist in ERP and commerce systems, but they are not automatically converted into advertising decisions.

## Target Users

Primary users:

- Ecommerce SME owners
- Growth marketers
- Marketplace sellers
- Small brand teams with limited creative resources

Secondary users:

- Agencies serving ecommerce SMEs
- Operations teams that manage product catalogs and stock

## Core Value Proposition

Kastonom turns commerce data into a ready-to-run AI video ad workflow:

1. Find the best product to promote.
2. Explain why it should be promoted now.
3. Generate an audience summary and creative brief.
4. Compile a provider-ready video prompt.
5. Send the prompt to a live provider or mock execution path through MCP network execution.
6. Create or mock a video generation job from the normalized provider result.
7. Show the result in a demo-ready creative console.

The user should not need to write prompts.

## Why Now

AI video providers can generate usable advertising assets, but ecommerce teams still need structured creative direction. Kastonom bridges operational commerce data and creative generation, making AI advertising practical for SMEs.

## Demo Scenario

A small ecommerce brand connects sample ERP/product data. Kastonom identifies a product with strong momentum or stock opportunity, generates a creative brief, compiles a video prompt, starts a mock or live video job, and displays the final ad preview in the frontend.

## MVP Scope

Included in MVP:

- Mock ERP/product dataset
- Product list and selected product view
- Product scoring with hype and trend potential signals
- Audience summary generation
- Structured creative brief generation
- Prompt compiler
- MCP network execution to live or mock providers
- Backend video job creation from normalized results
- Mock video provider fallback
- Frontend demo flow from product selection to ad preview

## Out-of-Scope Items

Not included in MVP unless a later contract explicitly adds them:

- Full ERP integration
- Full ecommerce platform OAuth
- Production billing
- Multi-language campaign optimization beyond configured prompt language
- Automated media buying
- Real-time trend scraping
- Long-running provider webhooks beyond the demo contract
- Advanced brand safety review

## Success Criteria

The MVP succeeds if:

- A judge can understand the product in under 60 seconds.
- The demo shows an end-to-end path from product data to ad preview.
- The system works without live provider credentials through fallback mode.
- AI behavior is constrained by explicit data and does not invent product facts.
- Each layer has clear boundaries and can be implemented by future agents from contracts.
